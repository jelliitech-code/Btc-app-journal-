Educational tool, not financial advice. Prices come from public exchange APIs (Binance, Hyperliquid, Deribit, Bybit, Coinbase) with automatic fallback and refresh every few seconds. The bias score and expected ranges are rule-based and statistical, not certain predictions. Swings, liquidity levels, FVGs, order blocks and the volume profile are simplified calculations on the loaded candles, so treat them as guides.
